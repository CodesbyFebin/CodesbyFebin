Principle: Measure, Then Verify, Then Admit

The operating sequence is: measure → verify → admit → execute → prove

Desired is not admitted. Admitted is not executing. Executing is not observed. Observed is not verified. Fail closed is the error policy. Missing signature, missing policy, unread journal, unreachable verifier: the action does not proceed.
